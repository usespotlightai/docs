This page is for you if someone has invited you to Spotlight by email. A
workspace is where an organisation keeps its work in Spotlight: its people, its
teams, and the tasks each team's coding agents work on. A team is a group of
people inside the workspace who share the same work.

You need a GitHub account whose email address is the one the invitation was
sent to. Spotlight signs you in with GitHub.

## Accept the invitation

<Steps>
<Step>

Open the email from Spotlight. Its subject names the person who invited you
and the workspace. Click **Accept the invitation**.

</Step>
<Step>

The page that opens says who invited you, to which workspace, and the role and
teams you join with. If you are not signed in, click **Continue with GitHub**
and approve the sign-in on GitHub. You come back to the same page.

</Step>
<Step>

Click **Accept invitation**.

</Step>
</Steps>

Spotlight opens the workspace's inbox. The inbox is where agents ask you
questions and where work waits for your answer. The teams you joined are listed
in the sidebar.

The link works only for you and for 14 days. Spotlight is served at
[app.usespotlight.ai](https://app.usespotlight.ai). If your organisation runs its
own Spotlight, the link points at its
[address](/docs/reference/glossary#spotlight-address) instead, and the steps are
the same.

## If the page says something else

| The page says | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Invitation is for &lt;invited address&gt;, not &lt;your address&gt;. Please sign in as &lt;invited address&gt; to accept." | You are signed in with another address. Click **Log out**, in the workspace menu at the top of the sidebar or on the page that asks you to create a workspace. Then open the link again and sign in with a GitHub account that uses the invited address. Or ask for an invitation to the address you use. |
| "This invitation has expired. Please request a new one." | Its 14 days ran out. Ask the person who invited you to invite you again. |
| "This invitation is revoked." | The invitation was taken back. Ask the person who invited you. |
| "This invitation is already accepted." | It has been used. Sign in at [app.usespotlight.ai](https://app.usespotlight.ai) to reach the workspace. |
| "Invitation link doesn't work" | The link is wrong or only partly copied. Open it from the email again, or ask for a new one. |

If you were already in the workspace, accepting keeps your role and adds you to
the teams the invitation names.
