The web app is served at `https://app.usespotlight.ai`, or at the
deployment's address on a [self-hosted Spotlight](/docs/reference/glossary#spotlight-address). Every page except `/login` and
`/invites/<token>` is behind sign-in. Routes below are relative to the
Spotlight address; `<slug>` is the workspace's slug and `<teamId>` a team's id.

The web app shows configuration read-only. Lines, stations, skills,
integrations, hooks, harness profiles, secrets, pools and trusts are written
through the [resource API](/docs/reference/api/resource-api) or the
[Terraform provider](/docs/reference/terraform/terraform-provider). There are
two exceptions: the Add a host page creates pull pools, and the Tokens page
issues and revokes workspace tokens.

## Navigation

The sidebar holds, from the top:

| Item | Leads to |
| --- | --- |
| The workspace menu | "Settings"; "Invite and manage members", which opens the Members page, for an owner or admin; the signed-in email, a switch between the person's workspaces, "Create or join a workspace" and "Log out". |
| "Inbox" | The inbox, with the number of unread items. |
| "Your teams" | Each team the person can see. A team opens to "Agent", "Lines" (one entry per line), "Runs", "Insights" and "Memory". |

"Settings" in the workspace menu opens the settings area, which has a sidebar
of its own. A red dot beside "Settings" means no host is answering.

| Item | Leads to |
| --- | --- |
| "Back to app" | The page the person left the app from, or the inbox. |
| "Workspace" | "General"; "Members", shown only to an owner or admin; "Runners", with the number of hosts answering; and "Tokens", shown only to an owner or admin. |
| Each team the person can see | "General", "Integrations" and "Skills". |

## Pages outside a workspace

| Route | Page | Shows and changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/login` | Log in | "Continue with GitHub". |
| `/workspaces/new` | Create workspace | "Log out" ("Logging out…" while it works) and "Logged in as:" with the email, above two steps. "Create workspace": a field with the placeholder "Workspace name", and the workspace URL after `usespotlight.ai/w/`, filled from the name until it is changed; "Continue" is turned off until both are filled. "Name your first team": a field with the placeholder "Product", then "Create workspace" ("Creating workspace…" while it works), or "Back". The fields' labels, "Workspace name", "Workspace URL" and "Team name", are read only by screen readers. See [A workspace created in the web app](/docs/reference/terraform/workspace#a-workspace-created-in-the-web-app). |
| `/invites/<token>` | Accept an invite | "&lt;inviter&gt; invited you to &lt;workspace&gt;", with the role and teams the invite gives ("You join as a member, on the &lt;team&gt; team."). Signed out: "Continue with GitHub", which returns to the page. Signed in with the invited address: "Accept invitation", which joins the workspace and opens its inbox. Signed in with another address: "Invitation is for &lt;invited address&gt;, not &lt;signed-in address&gt;. Please sign in as &lt;invited address&gt; to accept." A closed invite reads "Invitation already accepted.", "Invitation withdrawn." or "Invitation expired. Please ask for a new one."; a link that matches no invite reads "Invitation link doesn't work". See [Invites](/docs/reference/roles-and-permissions#invites). |
| `/github/installed` | GitHub App callback | Connects the installation to the workspace. See [Installation callback](/docs/reference/events/github-events#installation-callback). |

## Workspace pages

| Route | Page | Shows | Can change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `/w/<slug>/inbox` | Inbox | The person's conversations and notices, newest first, under "Open" and "Resolved". | Nothing on the list itself. |
| `/w/<slug>/inbox/notices/<noticeId>` | Notice | A [notice](/docs/reference/app/tasks-runs-and-the-inbox#notices): its headline, its points under "Details", and its link. | Nothing. Opening it settles it. |
| `/w/<slug>/inbox/<itemId>` | Inbox item | The task's conversation: notes, the runs that worked it and their steps. | Answer the conversation, with attachments. "Approve" or "Dismiss" an approval, edit a tool call's inputs before approving it, or copy the call. "Stop" a run still working the task. Grade a judged run (see [Grades](/docs/reference/app/tasks-runs-and-the-inbox#grades)). "Approve" or "Reject" each memory a run proposed, editing its text before approving it. Read a station alert that went off (see [Station alerts](/docs/reference/app/tasks-runs-and-the-inbox#station-alerts)). Connect an integration a tool needs to act as you (see [Requests to connect](/docs/reference/app/tasks-runs-and-the-inbox#requests-to-connect)). |
| `/w/<slug>/settings/runners` | Runners | One row per host serving the workspace and per push pool, under "Name", "Serves", "Status", "Images", "Harnesses", "Release" and "Last activity", filtered by "All", "Running", "Idle" or "Not answering". A host shows the pool or trust it came through, its standing ("Idle", "Running N of M" or "Not answering"), its default image with `+N` for the others and `N refused` for any it refused, the harnesses its images hold, its release, and `Up since` or `Last seen`. A push pool reads "Push pool", "Starting" or "Running N". Beneath each, one row per runner: the run it holds, or "No run yet", its standing ("Starting", "Running" or "Waiting on a person"), its image, its harnesses and when it started. A run of a team the viewer is not on shows a blurred title, with a tooltip saying it is hidden. | An owner or admin opens "Add a host", or "Add the first host" while there is no host. A member reads "Only workspace owners and admins can add hosts." |
| `/w/<slug>/settings/runners/new` | Add a host | Three steps: "Create a pool", "Add the machine to the &lt;key&gt; pool" and "Wait for the host to connect". The last step waits until a host of the new pool connects; the page then opens Runners. | An owner or admin creates a pull pool under "Pool name", which suggests `my-computer-1` or the next free number, and "Create pool". The page then shows the install command with the pool's token in it, once, with "Copy command". |
| `/w/<slug>/settings/members` | Members | Everyone in the workspace under "Members": name, email, teams ("No team" for none) and role. Under "Pending invites", each open invite: address, teams, "Invited by" and "Expires in N days" or "Expires within a day", and the role it gives. "Filter members" narrows the page with "Show": "Everyone", "Owners", "Admins", "Members" or "Pending invites". Shown to owners and admins only; a member who opens the route is sent to the workspace's own route. | "Invite people" opens the invite dialog: "Email addresses" (separated by commas or new lines), "Invite as" ("Admin" or "Member", the default) and "Teams", then "Send invites". The dialog then lists each address with "Invited", "Sent again", "Already a member" or "Not sent. Please resend it from Pending invites.", under a title that counts each outcome, as in "2 invited · 1 sent again", from "invited", "sent again", "already in the workspace" and "not sent", or "Invites" with none. "Done" closes it. In a member's actions menu, "Change role". In an invite's actions menu, "Resend invite" and "Revoke invite". |
| `/w/<slug>/settings/tokens` | Tokens | Each [workspace token](/docs/reference/api/workspace-token) a person issued: its name, when it was created, "No expiry", "Expires" with the date, or "Expired", and its scopes. Never the token itself. A member who opens the route is sent to the workspace's own route. | An owner or admin can "Create a token" with "Name", "What it is for", "What it may do" and "Expires after". "What it may do" is one of "Read", "Plan" or "Configure" (the default), with "Secrets", "Runners" and "Token trusts" beside it; "Expires after" is "30 days", "90 days" (the default), "1 year" or "Never". The page then shows the token, once, with "Copy token"; "I've copied it" dismisses it. "Revoke token" in a token's actions menu, then "Revoke token" again, revokes one. |

See [The inbox](/docs/reference/app/tasks-runs-and-the-inbox#the-inbox) for what
an inbox item holds and who may answer it.

## Team pages

All team routes start with `/w/<slug>/team/<teamId>`, except a team's
settings pages, which start with `/w/<slug>/settings/team/<teamId>`. The
team's own route opens its first line, or "Runs" when it has none.

| Route | Page | Shows | Can change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `…/agent` | Agent | The person's chats with the team's agent, each with its harness, its title, a working indicator while the agent works, and when it last changed. With none, "No chats yet". | Start a chat in the composer that reads "What do you want to plan?": pick the harness, model and effort, pick repositories, and attach files. Shared traces and a station to improve arrive in the composer only from the pages that open it with them, such as the Insights "Traces" tab and a station's "Improve" tab. See [Chats](/docs/reference/app/tasks-runs-and-the-inbox#chats). |
| `…/agent/<chatId>` | Chat | The chat's messages, the agent's steps and answers, and the tasks it proposed, as cards (see [Proposals](/docs/reference/app/tasks-runs-and-the-inbox#proposals)). The first message names the station the chat improves, linked to it. While the agent works, "The agent is working…". | Reply in the composer that reads "Reply…", with attachments, and change the model and effort for the reply. "Stop" the agent while it works. "Create task" or "Dismiss" a proposal, or copy it. |
| `…/lines/<lineId>` | Board | The line's stations as sections, with the tasks standing at each. The views "Backlog" (the backlog station), "Active" (work stations, the default) and "Completed" (the completed station). A task shows "Waiting for you" or that it is in progress. | Create a task under "New task" (title, description, attachments, "Create more", "Create task"). Reorder tasks within a station by dragging. Start selected backlog tasks, or park selected tasks, with "Move to". |
| `…/lines/<lineId>/stations/<stationId>` | Station | The station's name and description. A backlog or completed station shows only that nothing runs there. A work station has the tabs "Overview", the default, and "Improve"; another tab reads "Station not found". "Overview" shows "Latest runs on this config", over the config in force (see [Station outcomes](/docs/reference/app/tasks-runs-and-the-inbox#station-outcomes)). Then "Config": "Agent", with the harness, model and effort, or "No agent" and `Please pick a harness in Terraform.`; "Prompt", or `No prompt.`; "Skills", with their count and names, or "None"; "Judge" (see [Judge and alerts](/docs/reference/app/tasks-runs-and-the-inbox#judge-and-alerts)); "Ships through", with the line and its repositories; `Alert rules · <n>`, when the station has alerts; and "Version". Then "Runs": "Recent runs". "Version" and "Recent runs" read the newest group of runs with a recorded config, whether its runs started or not. | "See in Insights" and the version's "History" open the station on Insights (see [One station](/docs/reference/app/insights#one-station)), and the judge's "How it agrees with people" opens it at `#judge`. "All runs" opens the Insights Runs tab at the station. "View pull request" opens the pull request a picked search candidate opened for that version. |
| `…/lines/<lineId>/stations/<stationId>/improve` | Station: Improve | For a work station: "What stands out", with "Worth improving": the station's signals that got worse over the past 30 days or that trail the rest of the team, as under "Stations worth improving" on [Insights](/docs/reference/app/insights#overview). With none, it reads `Every signal looks healthy over the past 30 days` and `There's always room to improve. Talk it through with the agent.` Then "Try a change", with "Replay sets", at `#replays` (see [Station page](/docs/reference/app/replays#station-page)), and "Searches", at `#searches` (see [Station page](/docs/reference/app/searches#station-page)). | "Ask the agent" beside a signal opens the Agent page with the station attached to a new chat and the message `<signal> <now> (was <before>, all stations <all>). What should change at this station?` written, leaving out a comparison it lacks. With no signal listed, "Review with the agent" opens it with the station attached. "Create a replay set" asks for a name, then opens the new set's page. "Start a search" starts a search over one of the station's sets. |
| `…/lines/<lineId>/stations/<stationId>/replay-sets/<setId>` | Replay set | Under "Replays", the set's replays with their state, success, gain over the baseline and spend; under "Health", its noise, room to improve, judge and checks readings; under "Runs", how its runs turned out, each run and whether it can still be replayed; under "Checks", the checks replayed on it. See [Replay set page](/docs/reference/app/replays#replay-set-page). | "Rename" or "Delete" the set. Turn "Guards config changes" on or off. "Add runs" from the station's latest ended runs, with runs suggested to even the mix, or take one out. "Start a replay" under a candidate or the live config, within a ceiling, replaying each run 1 to 5 times. |
| `…/lines/<lineId>/stations/<stationId>/searches/<searchId>` | Search | A header with `Search <n>`, a pill for some endings, the replay set's name, linked, and when it ended, or started while it has not ended; tiles for the best candidate, the live config, iterations, tasks and spend; a `Why it stalled (iteration <n>)` card for each reflection; the "Candidates" card, each iteration's candidates with their scores, gains, cost and pull request, and a candidate's details beside it; and "Success against cost". See [Search page](/docs/reference/app/searches#search-page). | "Stop" a search under way. "Pick" a candidate to open a pull request. |
| `…/lines/<lineId>/stations/<stationId>/replays/<replayId>` | Replay | A header with the candidate's name, a "Stopped", "Over budget" or "Baseline" pill, the set's name, linked, `from its search` for a search's replay, and when it ended, or started while it has not ended; tiles for the replay's success and what scored it, its gain over the replay it is compared with, its side-by-side wins and what it spent; then "Tasks", each task's result against that replay, regressions first, and a task's runs, reasons, checks and changes beside it. See [Replay page](/docs/reference/app/replays#replay-page). | "Stop" a queued or running replay. |
| `…/tasks/<taskId>` | Task | Title, description ("No description" without one), attachments, and "Activity": every run of the task. A run that put the task to a person is led by "Waiting for you", or by "Answered" once the conversation is answered or closed, linked to its inbox item. Under "Properties": the station, the assignee, "Changes" when the task has a change stack, "View traces" when it has runs, and "Pull request" when it has one. | Start, park or close the task with the station property. Assign it. "View traces" opens the task trace. "Delete task" from "Task options", then "Delete task" again, or "Keep it". |
| `…/runs` | Runs | The team's runs, grouped by age, each with its task, its station, its state and when it started (see [Run states](/docs/reference/app/tasks-runs-and-the-inbox#run-states)). Filtered by "All" or "Waiting for you". With no runs, "No runs yet" and `A run starts when a station picks up a task. Create a task to start one.`, with a link to create a task. | Nothing. |
| `…/runs/<runId>` | Run | The run's turn of the task's conversation: the task's notes from after the previous run's last note to this run's last note, or to the latest note while this run holds the task, with the run's steps as they happen and the files it changed. The briefing heads it, unless the turn opens with what a person said. While the turn holds no notes, the page shows `Ran in <image>` and the briefing, or the task's title and description. Once the run is not working, a toolbar: "Copy the conversation"; the "Remembered" icon, when the run was given or proposed memories, which opens a pane with "Given to the agent", the memories it was briefed with or recalled, and "Proposed by this run", each with its kind and handle, and a proposal with "Kept", "Kept, now retired", "Rejected" or "Waiting for you", which links to the inbox item while it waits there; and when the run last spoke. A run that is not working, waiting or parked, whose turn holds no notes and no steps, reads `The run failed before the agent started.` or `The run ended before the agent started.`, with "Open the task". A replay run or a search's agent run shows what it ran for in place of a conversation, and its composer is turned off (see [Replay runs](/docs/reference/app/replays#replay-runs)). | Say something to the run, with attachments, answer its question, or send a finished station's work back to it. "Approve" or "Dismiss" an approval, edit a tool call's inputs before approving it, or copy the call; "Dismiss" first asks for a reason to tell the agent, which is optional (see [Approvals](/docs/reference/app/tasks-runs-and-the-inbox#approvals)). "Stop" a run a runner holds. In "Remembered", "This was wrong" on a standing memory asks why it no longer holds, then "Retire" retires it. Nothing on a replay run or a search's agent run. |
| `…/runs/<runId>/changes` | Change stack | The run's change stack: cohorts, layers, each file's changes and notes, and the decisions on each file. | Comment on files and lines, then "Finish review" with a summary and "Comment", "Approve" or "Request changes". The review posts to the task's pull request, or once the task opens one. Dispute a decision by replying to it. |
| `…/tasks/<taskId>/trace` | Task trace | One task's trace on a time scale: a row for each run, `<Station>` or `<Station> · attempt <n>`, or "Run" for a run with no station, with the judge's score beside a judged run when it is shown (see [Judge score](/docs/reference/app/tasks-runs-and-the-inbox#judge-score)); under it the phases "Prepare", `Turn · <harness>` and "Publish", and the run's steps and spans. With no runs, "No runs yet" and `The trace fills in after a station works this task.` | Fold or unfold a row. "Open the task" when it has no runs. |
| `…/memory` | Memory | "Filter memories" and "Write a memory" in the page header, then the team's [memories](/docs/reference/glossary#memory) in a table with the columns "Memory", "Kind", "Applies to", "Source", "Kept by" and "Runs", under the tabs "Standing" and "Retired" (`?standing=retired`). Standing memories are listed newest kept first, retired ones newest retired first, at most 500. "Filter memories" narrows them by "Kind", "Line", "Station", "Repository" and "Path", as the query parameters `kind`, `lineId`, `stationId`, `repository` and `path`. "Station" lists the chosen line's work stations, and is off until a line is chosen. "Repository" lists the chosen line's repositories, or those of every line. "Path" offers the paths of the memories listed, and matches every memory with a path that starts with the chosen one. "Kind" shows what the kind means on hover. "Applies to" reads "The whole team", or the line, station, repository and paths, or "Line deleted". "Source" reads "Person", "Run" or "Outside the team". "Kept by" names the person who approved or wrote it, or reads "Someone". "Runs" counts the runs briefed with it, that recalled it, or that read its file. Selecting a row expands it. In the "Source" column it then shows what the memory rests on: each note with a person's name, "A teammate" or "From another tool", and each step with the agent that ran it, or "The agent"; then "Open its run" and "Open its task". "Replaces:" gives the memory it replaced. A retired memory shows who retired it, when and why, or "A newer memory replaced this one." with "Replaced by:" and the newer memory. `?memory=<memoryId>` expands that memory's row. With no standing memories and no filter, "No memories yet", `Stations that keep memories propose them after runs. Approve them in the inbox, or write one yourself.` and "Write a memory". With none to list otherwise, "No memories match.", or "No retired memories match." under "Retired". | "Write a memory" (`?write=1`) adds a row at the top: the memory ("What the team should remember", at most 1,000 characters), "Kind" ("Preference" at first), "Line" ("The whole team"), "Station" ("Every station"), "Repository" ("Any repository") and "Paths, separated by commas", then "Save" or "Cancel". The row's "Source" reads "Person", "Kept by" your name, and "Runs" `0`. On an expanded standing memory, "Edit" opens the same fields in the row. In both, "Station" is off until a line is chosen and the paths until a repository is, and choosing a line clears the station and repository. "Save" is off while the memory is empty and reads "Saving…" while it saves. Saving an edit keeps it as a new memory with a new handle and the source "Person", in place of the old one, which moves to "Retired". "Retire" asks "Why it no longer holds", at most 500 characters, then "Retire"; runs are no longer given it. In the memory and the reason, Cmd+Enter or Ctrl+Enter saves or retires, and Escape cancels. |
| `…/insights` | Insights | How the team's runs, tasks and pull requests went over a period, against the period before, under "Overview", "Cost", "People", "Productivity", "Stations", "Tools and skills" and "Runs", and the team's task traces under "Traces" (`?tab=traces`). One station's figures, failures, runs by config and judge agreement at `?tab=stations&stationId=<stationId>`. See [Insights](/docs/reference/app/insights). | The period. "Chart" or "Table" on a card that has both. On "Traces", select traces and "Discuss with the agent". "Improve" on a station worth improving opens the Agent page with the station attached. "Impact" on a config change, a diamond or a group of runs by config opens a side panel. |
| `…/settings/general` | General | "Team name", and "Members" with their roles. | An owner or admin can "Add a member": choose someone under "In the workspace" who is not on the team, or "Invite someone new", which opens the invite dialog with the team ticked. Change a member's role. |
| `…/settings/integrations` | Integrations | "GitHub", then each integration the team defines, one row each. Selecting a row opens its details. Never a token. The GitHub row reads "Not installed on this team's repositories", "Works in this team's repositories" while its repositories load, or `Reaches <n> repositories` (`Reaches 1 repository` for one). A warning icon beside its name, labelled "Repositories not reached", shows when a repository the team's lines use is out of the App's reach; its hover reads `The App can't reach <repositories>.` Its details: "App", "Installed" or "Not installed", with `Agents read code and open pull requests through it.`; once installed, "Reaches", the repositories the App reaches, or "No repositories"; and, when the team's lines use repositories, "Lines use", each one out of reach with a "Not reached" warning icon whose hover reads `The App can't reach <repository>. Agents can't work in it. Please give the App access on GitHub.` An integration's row reads "Receives events" when it has no `connection.oauth2`, else "Acts as the team", or "Acts as each person" when its `connection.holder` is `requester`, followed by `· <n> tools` (`· 1 tool` for one) when it has an API description or MCP server read without a problem. A failed read shows a warning icon labelled "Could not be read", whose hover reads `Its <OpenAPI description, GraphQL schema or MCP server> <problem>.` Its details: "Team account", for an integration with `connection.oauth2`, with the account's handle, "Connected" or "Not connected", and `Watches and posts for the team.` for a `requester` integration, else `Agents' tools act as this account.`; "Your account", for a `requester` integration, with the person's own handle, "Connected" or "Not connected", and `Its tools act as you on tasks you start.`; "Acting as you", a rule for each tool, once the person is connected and the integration offers tools; "People", for a `requester` integration and only to owners and admins, who has connected, or "Nobody has connected yet", and `Not connected: <names>`; "Tools", `<n> tools from its <OpenAPI description, GraphQL schema or MCP server>, read <when>` (`1 tool` for one), where `<when>` is "just now", minutes or hours ago as in "5m ago", or the day after 24 hours, or the problem; and "Defined in", where the integration is defined. | While a row is closed, its action shows on the row: "Install on GitHub" when GitHub is not installed; "Connect team account" to an owner or admin while the team account is not connected, and "Waiting for an admin" to anyone else; and "Connect your account" to a person not yet connected to a `requester` integration. In the details, "Install on GitHub" or "Manage on GitHub"; an owner or admin can "Connect team account", or "Change account" from the team account's actions menu; anyone can "Connect your account", or "Disconnect" from their account's actions menu, and set each tool's calls made with their connection to "Allow", "Ask me first" or "Block". While a connection opens, its button reads "Opening…". A failure reads `Could not connect. Try again.` or `Could not disconnect. Try again.` See [Connect an integration that signs in with OAuth](/docs/integrations/connect-an-integration#connect-yourself) and [Let a person ask first or block a tool](/docs/integrations/require-approval-before-a-tool-runs#let-a-person-ask-first-or-block-a-tool). |
| `…/settings/skills` | Skills | The team's skills. | Nothing. |

invite, change roles and connect GitHub, and
[Change stacks and decisions](/docs/reference/app/change-stacks-and-decisions) for
what a change stack holds.
