title: Invite people and change their roles
description: Invite people to the workspace by email with a role and teams, follow up on pending invites, change a member's role, and add a member to a team.
---

You need to be an owner or admin of the workspace. People are invited to the
workspace, as an admin or a member, and can be put on teams as they join.
[Roles and permissions](/docs/reference/roles-and-permissions) lists what each
role may do.

## Invite people

<Steps>
<Step>

Open the workspace menu at the top of the sidebar and choose
**Invite and manage members**. You can also choose **Settings**, then
**Members** under **Workspace**.

</Step>
<Step>

Click **Invite people**.

</Step>
<Step>

Under **Email addresses**, enter one or more addresses, separated by commas or
new lines. You can invite up to 50 at once.

</Step>
<Step>

Under **Invite as**, choose **Member** or **Admin**. Everyone in this round is
invited with the same role. Nobody can be invited as an owner: invite them as
an admin, then [change their role](#change-a-role) once they have joined.

</Step>
<Step>

Under **Teams**, tick the teams they should join. Leave it as **No team** to
invite them to the workspace only.

</Step>
<Step>

Click **Send invites**.

</Step>
</Steps>

**Invites sent** lists each address with what happened to it:

| Outcome | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| **Invited** | An invite was emailed to the address. |
| **Sent again** | The address already had a pending or expired invite. It was emailed again with a new link, the role and teams you chose this time, and another 14 days. |
| **Already a member** | The address belongs to someone in the workspace. Nothing was sent. To put them on a team, [add them to it](#add-a-member-to-a-team). |
| **Not sent &mdash; please try again** | The email could not be sent. Invite the address again. |

Click **Done**. The new invites appear under **Pending invites** on the
**Members** page until they are accepted, revoked or expire.

If an address under **Email addresses** is not an email address, the dialog
names it under the field and sends nothing. Correct or remove it.

## Follow up on a pending invite

Under **Pending invites**, each row shows the address, its teams, who invited
them and when the invite expires. Open the actions menu at the end of the row:

- **Resend invite** emails a new link with another 14 days. The old link stops
  working.
- **Revoke invite** takes the invite back. Its link stops working.

An invite that expires leaves the list. To try again, invite the address
again; the outcome reads **Sent again**.

To see only the pending invites, open **Filter members** and set **Show** to
**Pending invites**. The same filter shows only **Owners**, **Admins** or
**Members**.

## Change a role

On the **Members** page, open the actions menu at the end of a person's row,
choose **Change role**, then **Owner**, **Admin** or **Member**. You can also
change a role from the role beside a person's name on a team's **General**
page.

If a person's row has no actions menu, you may not change their role:

- An admin can change only members, and can make them admins but not owners.
- An owner can change anyone, including other owners.
- The last owner of a workspace cannot be given another role. Make someone
  else an owner first.

## Add a member to a team

<Steps>
<Step>

Open the workspace menu, choose **Settings**, and under the team's name click
**General**.

</Step>
<Step>

Click **Add a member**. Under **In the workspace**, choose the person.

</Step>
</Steps>

The menu lists only people in the workspace who are not on the team. To bring
in someone new, choose **Invite someone new** instead: the invite dialog opens
with the team already ticked under **Teams**.

## What the person you invite does

The person gets an email from Spotlight whose subject names you and the
workspace, with an **Accept the invitation** button. They open it, click
**Continue with GitHub** if they are not signed in, then
**Accept invitation**, and land in the workspace's inbox. They must sign in
with the address you invited.
[Join a workspace from an invitation](/docs/getting-started/join-a-workspace-from-an-invitation)
walks them through it.

When they accept, they join the workspace with the role you chose and the
teams you ticked that still exist. Someone already in the workspace keeps their
role and is added to the teams.

An invite lasts 14 days. If it runs out, invite the address again or click
**Resend invite** while it is still pending.

<Callout>
On a self-hosted Spotlight, invites are emailed through the
[mail server](/docs/reference/self-hosted-settings#mail) the deployment is set
up with. Without one, the message is written to the control
plane's log instead, and the outcome still reads **Invited**.
</Callout>
