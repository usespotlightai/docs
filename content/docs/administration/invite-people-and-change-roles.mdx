People are invited to a team, and their role applies across the whole
workspace. You need to be an owner or admin of the workspace.

## Invite someone

<Steps>
<Step>

In the sidebar, under **Your teams**, expand the team, then **Settings**, and
click **General**.

</Step>
<Step>

Click **Add a member**, enter the person's email address, and click
**Send invitation**.

</Step>
</Steps>

The invitation appears under **Invited** while it waits to be accepted. It
lasts 14 days.

The person opens the link in the email and clicks **Continue with GitHub**,
signing in with a GitHub account whose email address is the one you invited.
They then click **Accept invitation** and land on the team. If they are signed
in with another address, the page names both addresses and asks them to sign in
as the invited one.

A person new to the workspace joins it as a **Member**. Someone already in the
workspace keeps their role.

If their invitation has run out, send another.

## Take an invitation back

Under **Invited**, click the **Unsend** button beside the address, a small
round icon that turns into an undo arrow when you point at it. The link stops
working, and the page it opens says the invitation has been taken back.

## Change someone's role

Under **Members**, choose the role from the dropdown beside the person's name:
**Owner**, **Admin** or **Member**. [Roles and permissions](/docs/reference/roles-and-permissions) lists
what each role may do.

If there is no dropdown beside a person, you may not change their role:

- An admin can change only members, and can make them admins but not owners.
- An owner can change anyone, including other owners.
- The last owner of a workspace cannot be given another role. Make someone else
  an owner first.
