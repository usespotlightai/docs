title: Add another email to your account

---

Spotlight knows you by the email you sign in with and by each additional email
you verify. A mention of Spotlight in Slack is yours when your Slack account
uses one of these addresses. A task that an integration assigns with
[`assignBy.email`](/docs/reference/terraform/integration#raise) goes to you when the
email is one of them. So does a task that an event opens without a person on
a line whose
[`default_assignee`](/docs/reference/terraform/line#line-fields) is one of
them. A reply in Slack that answers a task shows your avatar. You need to be able to read mail sent to the address you
add.

## Add an email

<Steps>
<Step>

Open the workspace menu at the top of the sidebar and choose **Settings**, then
**Emails** under **Account**.

</Step>
<Step>

Under **Additional emails**, click **Add email**.

</Step>
<Step>

Enter the address under **Email** and click **Send verification link**. The
address shows "Not verified", and Spotlight sends a message with the subject
"Verify your email for Spotlight" to it.

</Step>
<Step>

Open the link in the message while you are signed in to Spotlight as the same
account. If you are signed out, the page asks you to sign in first. The page
shows **Email verified**, and the address on the Emails page shows "Verified".

</Step>
</Steps>

The link works for 24 hours. You can have up to 10 additional emails. Your
sign-in email cannot be one of them.

## Send a new link

If the link expired or did not arrive, open the address's actions menu on the
Emails page and choose **Resend verification link**. The new link replaces the
earlier one. Spotlight sends a link to an address at most once a minute, and
at most 10 links for your account in an hour. Links to addresses you removed
are in that count.

## Remove an email

Open the address's actions menu on the Emails page and choose
**Remove email**. Spotlight then stops matching that address to you. A Slack
mention from an account with that address is refused, and a task assigned by
that address goes to the line's default assignee, or opens with no assignee.

## If the address is not verified

- If the page says the address belongs to another Spotlight account, another
  account signs in with it or has verified it. An address can belong to one
  account only. Remove it there first, or sign in as that account.
- If the page says another account added the address, you opened the link
  while signed in as a different account. Sign in as the account that added it,
  then open the link again.
- If the page says the link has expired, send a new link.

[Errors and messages](/docs/reference/errors-and-messages#emails-page-and-verifying-an-email)
lists every message.
