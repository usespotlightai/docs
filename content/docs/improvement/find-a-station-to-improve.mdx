title: Find a station to improve
description: Use the team's Insights page to find the station whose runs got worse or trail the rest of the team, and see why.
---

Use this when you want to know which of the team's stations to work on first.
Only people on the team and the workspace's owners and admins can see the
team's Insights.

<Steps>
<Step>

In the sidebar, open the team and select **Insights**. Choose **Past 7 days**,
**Past 30 days** or **Past 90 days** in the period picker.

</Step>
<Step>

On **Overview**, read **Stations worth improving**. Each station listed got
worse than in the period before, or trails the rest of the team, on the
signals named under it, such as **Sent back** or **Cost per run**. The first
station is worse on the most signals. If the card reads **Nothing stands out
this period.**, try a longer period.

</Step>
<Step>

Select the station's runs count to see those runs on the **Runs** tab. Narrow
them with **Outcome**, and select an outcome to open a run.

</Step>
<Step>

To see where the station's runs fail, open **Stations** and find its row. It
lists its task failures and its failed runs by reason. To see where its tasks
wait, open **Flow**.

</Step>
<Step>

When you know what to change, select **Improve this station** on the
**Overview** card, and follow
[Improve a station from its runs](/docs/improvement/improve-a-station-from-its-runs).

</Step>
</Steps>

A diamond under a day of **Output against cost** marks a station starting a new
config. Select it to see the runs with that config.
