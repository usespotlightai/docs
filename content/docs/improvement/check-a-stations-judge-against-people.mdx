Use this once a work station has a judge, to get its verdicts shown or to find
out why they are not. To give a station a judge, see
[Set a station's judge and alerts](/docs/improvement/set-a-stations-judge-and-alerts).

Only people on the team and the workspace's owners and admins can grade runs
and see the judge's agreement.

## Grade judged runs

Some judged runs reach the inbox as **A judged run needs your grade. Grades
check the judge.** They reach the task's assignee, or everyone on the team when the
task has no assignee. Open one. It shows the run's conversation, then a card
with the judge's claims.

Under **Does the run meet each claim?**, select **Yes** or **No** beside every
claim, then select **Save grade**. To change it, change an answer and select
**Save grade** again.

Nothing waits on your grade.

## Read the judge's agreement with people

<Steps>
<Step>

Open the line's board and select the station's name. On the station's page,
open the **Judge** tab. Under **Agreement with people**, the tab shows the
current claims.

</Step>
<Step>

If the judge has judged runs under more than one version of the claims, pick
the one to read under **Claims**.

</Step>
<Step>

Read the line under **Agreement with people**. It says **Judge verdicts show on
runs.** or **Judge verdicts are hidden on runs.** If the verdicts are hidden, hover
over the line to see why:

- If it reads **They show when 10 runs have a verdict from the judge and from
  people.**, grade more of the station's runs, or wait for people to accept or
  send back more of them. **Runs with both verdicts** shows how many have both.
- If it reads **Agreement cannot be measured while every verdict is the
  same.**, grade more runs until both verdicts vary.
- If it reads **Cohen’s kappa is below the minimum**, follow
  [Change the claims or the minimum agreement](#change-the-claims-or-the-minimum-agreement).

</Step>
<Step>

Look under **Agreement by claim**. If one claim's kappa is far below the
others, reword that claim. These figures come from grades only.

</Step>
</Steps>

[Judge tab](/docs/reference/app/tasks-runs-and-the-inbox#judge-tab) says what
each tile and part shows, and
[Agreement](/docs/reference/app/tasks-runs-and-the-inbox#agreement) how
agreement is measured.

## Change the claims or the minimum agreement

Under **Judge compared with people**, compare the judge's verdicts, in rows, with
people's, in columns. If the counts off the diagonal lean one way, the judge is
stricter or looser than people:

- To make the judge read the station as people do, reword, add or remove a
  claim in the station's `judge` block, or change `pass_threshold`, and run
  `terraform apply`. The new claims version starts with no runs that have both
  verdicts. Once the judge has judged runs under it, it is the
  **Current claims** under **Claims**.
- To show the verdicts at a lower agreement, lower `min_agreement` in the station's
  `judge` block and run `terraform apply`.
