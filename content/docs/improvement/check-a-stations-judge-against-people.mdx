title: Check a station's judge against people
description: Read the judge's agreement with people on the station's Judge tab, answer judge disagreements from the inbox, and change the claims or the minimum agreement when its verdicts stay hidden.
---

Use this once a work station has a judge, to get its verdicts shown or to find
out why they are not. To give a station a judge, see
[Set a station's judge and alerts](/docs/improvement/set-a-stations-judge-and-alerts).

Only people on the team and the workspace's owners and admins can see the
judge's agreement.

People's verdicts come from each run's
[deciding action](/docs/reference/glossary#deciding-action): the first start,
park, accept or send-back after the run, or a hold on the task that ends with
the reason **The line got it wrong**. An accept passes the run. A send-back or
such a hold fails it. A hold resumed with **I want to try something** is no
deciding action. When the deciding action is none of those, a merged pull
request passes the station's last completed run on the task. An answer to the
run is no verdict. Accepting a run that failed, ran out,
was cancelled or was superseded gives no verdict either. The judge never judges
such a run, so it never counts in agreement. Nobody is asked to give a
verdict.

## Read the judge's agreement with people

<Steps>
<Step>

Open the line's board and select the station's name. On the station's page,
open the **Judge** tab. Under **Agreement with people**, the tab shows the
current claims.

</Step>
<Step>

If the judge has judged runs under more than one version of the claims, pick
the one to read under **Claims**.

</Step>
<Step>

Read the line under **Agreement with people**. It says **Judge verdicts show on
runs.** or **Judge verdicts are hidden on runs.** If the verdicts are hidden, hover
over the line to see why:

- If it reads **They show when 10 runs have a verdict from the judge and from
  people.**, wait for people to act on more of the station's runs: accept
  them, send them back, put their tasks on hold, or merge their pull requests.
  A hold counts only when it ends with the reason **The
  line got it wrong**. **Runs with both verdicts** shows how many have both.
- If it reads **Agreement cannot be measured while every verdict is the
  same.**, wait for more runs until both verdicts vary.
- If it reads **Cohen’s kappa is below the minimum**, follow
  [Change the claims or the minimum agreement](#change-the-claims-or-the-minimum-agreement).

</Step>
</Steps>

[Judge tab](/docs/reference/app/tasks-runs-and-the-inbox#judge-tab) says what
each tile and part shows, and
[Agreement](/docs/reference/app/tasks-runs-and-the-inbox#agreement) how
agreement is measured.

## Answer judge disagreements

When the deciding action on a run is your accept, your send-back, or your
hold once it ends with the reason **The line got it wrong**, and the judge gave
the other verdict, your inbox gets
**The judge and people disagree on a run. Spotlight asks why.** When a merged
pull request opened the disagreement, the item goes to the task's assignee, or
to everyone who can see the team when the task has no assignee. It stays there
when you act on the run later. Answer it as in
[Say why the judge and people disagree](/docs/inbox/answer-the-inbox#say-why-the-judge-and-people-disagree).
The answers do not change the judge's agreement with people. They go into a
[claims revision](/docs/reference/app/tasks-runs-and-the-inbox#claims-revisions),
which can open a pull request with new claims.

## Change the claims or the minimum agreement

Under **Judge compared with people**, compare the judge's verdicts, in rows, with
people's, in columns. If the counts off the diagonal lean one way, the judge is
stricter or looser than people:

- To make the judge read the station as people do, reword, add or remove a
  claim in the station's `judge` block, or change `pass_threshold`, and run
  `terraform apply`. The new claims version starts with no runs that have both
  verdicts. Once the judge has judged runs under it, it is the
  **Current claims** under **Claims**.
- To show the verdicts at a lower agreement, lower `min_agreement` in the station's
  `judge` block and run `terraform apply`.
