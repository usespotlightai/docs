Use this once a work station has a judge, to get its scores shown or to find
out why they are not. To give a station a judge, see
[Set a station's judge and alerts](/docs/improvement/set-a-stations-judge-and-alerts).

Only people on the team and the workspace's owners and admins can grade runs
and see the judge's agreement.

## Grade judged runs

Some judged runs reach the inbox as **Grade a judged run, so the team can check
its judge.** They reach the task's assignee, or everyone on the team when the
task has no assignee. Open one. It shows the task, **What the run said last** and **Its
changes**.

Under **Is this a good run?**, select **Yes** or **No**. On a 1 to 5 rubric,
the question reads **How good is this run, from 1 to 5?**: select a number.
To change your grade, select another answer.

On a judge with claims, the question reads **Does the run meet each claim?**.
Select **Yes** or **No** beside every claim, then **Save grade**. To change
your grade, change any answer and select **Save grade** again.

Nothing waits on your grade.

## Read the judge's agreement with people

Open the line's board and select the station's name. Under **Judge**, the
first line says whether the judge is trusted:

- If it reads **Hidden until more runs have both a judge score and people's**,
  grade more of the station's runs. At least 10 need both scores.
- If it reads **Hidden: every score so far is the same**, grade more runs until
  both scores vary.
- If it reads **Hidden:** followed by the measure and **below the** trust it
  needs, compare the judge's scores with people's in the matrix on the
  **Current** rubric card, and follow the next section.
- If it reads **Hidden: the station's rubric names a scale that cannot be
  read**, correct the rubric's first line to `scores: yes-no` or
  `scores: 1-5`.

**Both scores**, **Judge only**, **People only** and **Neither** count the
station's recent runs by which scores they have.

On a judge with claims, the agreement is Cohen's kappa over every claim's
marks together. The **Current** rubric card lists each claim under
**Agreement claim by claim**, with its own kappa, the share agreed and the
runs it counts. If one claim's kappa is far below the others, reword that
claim.

## Change the rubric or the trust

The matrix on the **Current** rubric card sets the judge's scores, down,
against people's, across. If its counts off the diagonal lean one way, the
judge is stricter or looser than people:

- To make the judge read the station as people do, edit the rubric file, such
  as `judges/core/delivery/implementation.md`, or reword the claim people
  disagree with it on, and run `terraform apply`. The new rubric version gets
  a card of its own and starts with no runs that have both scores.
- To show the scores at a lower agreement, lower `trust` in the station's
  `judge` block and run `terraform apply`.
