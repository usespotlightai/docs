Use this once a work station has a judge, to get its scores shown or to find
out why they are not. To give a station a judge, see
[Set a station's judge and alerts](/docs/improvement/set-a-stations-judge-and-alerts).

Only people on the team and the workspace's owners and admins can grade runs
and see the judge's agreement.

## Grade judged runs

Some judged runs reach the inbox as **A judged run needs your grade. Grades
check the judge.** They reach the task's assignee, or everyone on the team when the
task has no assignee. Open one. It shows the run's conversation, then a card
with the question and, when the judge has a rubric, the rubric itself.

Under **Does this run meet the rubric?**, select **Yes** or **No**. On a 1 to 5
rubric, the question reads **How well does this run meet the rubric, from 1 to
5?**: select a number. Without a rubric, the questions read **Is this a good
run?** and **How good is this run, from 1 to 5?**. Your grade saves as soon as
you choose. To change it, select another answer.

On a judge with claims, the question reads **Does the run meet each claim?**.
Select **Yes** or **No** beside every claim. Your grade saves once every claim
is answered. To change it, change any answer.

Nothing waits on your grade.

## Read the judge's agreement with people

<Steps>
<Step>

Open the line's board and select the station's name. On the **Overview** tab,
under **Judge**, select **How it agrees with people**. The station's view on
Insights opens at **How far to trust the judge**.

</Step>
<Step>

If the judge has judged runs under more than one rubric version, pick the one
to read under **Rubric**.

</Step>
<Step>

Read the first tile, which says whether the judge is trusted:

- If it reads **Hidden until more runs have scores from both the judge and
  people**, grade more of the station's runs. At least 10 need both scores.
- If it reads **Hidden. Agreement can't be measured while all scores are
  identical.**, grade more runs until both scores vary.
- If it reads **Hidden.** followed by the measure and **Minimum required**,
  follow [Change the rubric or the trust](#change-the-rubric-or-the-trust).
- If it reads **Hidden. The rubric names a scale that cannot be read.**,
  correct the rubric's first line to `scores: yes-no` or `scores: 1-5`.

</Step>
<Step>

On a judge with claims, look under **By claim**. If one claim's kappa is far
below the others, reword that claim.

</Step>
</Steps>

[Agreement](/docs/reference/app/tasks-runs-and-the-inbox#agreement) says what
each tile, statistic and card shows.

## Change the rubric or the trust

Under **Score comparison**, compare the judge's scores, in rows, with
people's, in columns. If the counts off the diagonal lean one way, the judge is
stricter or looser than people:

- To make the judge read the station as people do, edit the rubric file, such
  as `judges/core/delivery/implementation.md`, or reword the claim people
  disagree with it on, and run `terraform apply`. The new rubric version starts
  with no runs that have both scores. Once the judge has judged runs under it,
  it is the **Current version** under **Rubric**.
- To show the scores at a lower agreement, lower `trust` in the station's
  `judge` block and run `terraform apply`.
